BEFORE YOU COME ABOARD

Know what to expect.

What does the published package include?

Cabin accommodation, sightseeing, meals and snacks across the trip, an experienced boat driver and guide, cook, and rooftop dining are named in the listing. Confirm your exact package before paying.

How long is the journey?

The listing describes a two-day, one-night tour starting and ending at Saheb Bari Ghat, Sunamganj.

How does payment work?

The booking concept is designed for full advance payment after room and date selection. This demo does not collect payment. Nautilus must approve the terms and payment channel.

What about weather or route changes?

Water level, weather and local conditions can change the route. Ask the operator how changes, cancellations and refunds are handled for your chosen date.
