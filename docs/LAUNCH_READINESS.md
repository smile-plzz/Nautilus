# Launch readiness

## Operator content approval

- [ ] Confirm official business and boat name, spelling, owner contact and authorized website operator.
- [ ] Approve description, story, route, duration, boarding point, menu and included/excluded items.
- [ ] Provide permission-cleared Nautilus photos and trip video, including usage rights.
- [ ] Confirm all room names, room capacities, guest-count rules and accessible amenities.
- [ ] Provide current dates, inventory, pricing and any seasonal/route conditions.
- [ ] Supply current certificates, registration and inspection records, with issuer and validity dates.
- [ ] Confirm crew names/titles and what may be published.
- [ ] Provide authentic review sources, permission to republish, dates and moderation contact.
- [ ] Approve booking, child, cancellation, weather disruption, refund and complaint terms.
- [ ] Define when a room is held, when payment is captured, when a booking becomes final, and how the owner receives booking details.

## Technical and operations before payment

- [ ] Add a Bangladesh-supported payment provider, merchant verification and secure webhook handling.
- [ ] Make payment success the only path to confirmed reservation; make payment retry and timeout behavior safe.
- [ ] Validate the exact amount and inventory again when receiving payment callbacks.
- [ ] Notify guest and operator, issue a receipt and share boarding instructions.
- [ ] Add cancellation/refund actions and reconcile payment-provider settlements.
- [ ] Add operator accounts, stronger authentication, audit history and backup/restore.
- [ ] Publish privacy notice, terms, contact channel and clear operator/platform responsibilities.
- [ ] Test on low-end Android, mobile networks, Bangla copy, keyboard and screen readers.
- [ ] Run a pilot with real operator-controlled inventory while phone/WhatsApp remain a fallback.
- [ ] Remove demo dates, demo labels and simulated-payment copy only after production sign-off.
